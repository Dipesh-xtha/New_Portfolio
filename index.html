At the award ceremonyUI/UX Showdown
First position

UI/UX Showdown

Design topicMaking eSewa pending payments easier to understand

The issue

Money may be deducted while the merchant has not received it. Users are left unsure whether to wait, retry, or seek help.

My solution

I explored clearer payment feedback, safeguards against duplicate payments, and visible support-case progress.

When6-hour challenge
EventICT Meetup v8.0
Organized byPrime IT Club
How I won — the short process +
  1. Frame: focus on the uncertainty after a payment is deducted but not received by the merchant.
  2. Clarify: make payment status and the safe next step easier to understand.
  3. Support: reduce accidental repeat payments and show how a support case is progressing.

I brought this journey together within the six-hour challenge, and the entry earned first position.
